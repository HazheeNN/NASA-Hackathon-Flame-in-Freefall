# Submission pack

1. Read `docs/submission.md` (template and limits), `docs/challenge.md` (the brief and judging criteria), `docs/status.md` (numbers), the top 5 insights in `data/generated/insights.json`, and the experiment list.
2. Fill the template with **real numbers and real insight titles** from the data. Never invent a number or a claim; anything not in the app doesn't go in the pitch.
3. Map each judging criterion to one concrete thing in the product (Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation).
4. Video script: fit the exact length limit; first 5 seconds state the problem; show the app, not slides; end on the URL.
5. Slides: at most the allowed number; one idea each; real screenshots (take them at 1920×1080).
6. List every NASA source used (from `data/experiments.json` and `data/findings.json` sources) for the "NASA data and resources" field. Include the AI disclosure from `docs/submission.md`.
7. Write the result back into `docs/submission.md` (replace the draft sections) and show the user a short summary and anything still missing.
